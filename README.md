## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Revenue – ₹19.80 Cr**  
  Total sales value generated across all customers, products, and regions.

- **Total Profit – ₹8.57 Cr**  
  Net profitability indicating overall business performance and cost efficiency.

- **Total Quantity Sold – 89.5 L units**  
  Total volume of products sold, useful for demand and inventory analysis.

- **Total Transactions – 2,28,265**  
  Number of sales orders processed, reflecting customer activity.

- **Total Customers – 402**  
  Unique customers contributing to overall revenue.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Revenue shows steady growth from 2013 to 2015, followed by a sharp decline in 2016, indicating potential market or operational challenges.
- Sales contribution varies significantly across employees, helping identify top performers and improvement areas.
- Revenue is concentrated in specific regions across North America, highlighting key geographic markets.
- Some customers generate high revenue but relatively lower profit, indicating pricing or cost optimization opportunities.
- Higher sales quantity does not always result in higher profit, suggesting differences in product margins.
